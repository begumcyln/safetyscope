# Regulatory Context (verified sources only)

The project does **not** assess legal compliance. Statements in alerts such as "prohibited in cosmetic products" are
the wording of the notifying authority and are attributed to Safety Gate. The two most frequent substances were
additionally checked against the official EU legal database (EUR-Lex), read on **27 November 2026**:

| Substance | Legal act | Adopted | Annex II (prohibited substances) entry | Applies from | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-(4-tert-butylbenzyl) propionaldehyde (BMHCA), CAS 80-54-6 | Commission Regulation (EU) 2021/1902, amending Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 (CMR substances) | 29 Oct 2021 | entry 1666 | 1 March 2022 | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/1902/oj |
| Diphenyl(2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl)phosphine oxide (TPO), CAS 75980-60-8 | Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/877, amending Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 (CMR substances) | 12 May 2025 | entry 1731 (previous Annex III entry 311 deleted) | 1 September 2025 | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/877/oj |

Notes
- According to recital 6 of Regulation (EU) 2025/877, TPO had previously been allowed (Annex III) for professional use
  in artificial nail systems at up to 5 %. The project does not assess whether individual TPO alerts concerned
  professional or consumer products, or how earlier alerts relate to the previous restriction; the timing of TPO
  alerts relative to 1 September 2025 is shown descriptively in the executive summary.
- Consolidated versions and later amendments were not checked.
- No other substance's legal status is asserted anywhere in this project.
