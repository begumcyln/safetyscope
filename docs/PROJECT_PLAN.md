# Project Plan — SafetyScope: European Cosmetics Safety Intelligence

## Business problem

Retailers, importers, brand owners and regulatory-affairs teams need to know which cosmetic products and ingredients
trigger EU safety alerts, where those alerts come from, and which corrective measures follow. The EU **Safety Gate**
portal publishes these alerts, but its exports are semi-structured: substances are buried in free text, several
fields hold multiple values, and the export has no date column. SafetyScope turns one export into a reproducible,
documented, queryable intelligence product.

## Guiding rules

- The original export is never modified (SHA-256 checked by an automated test).
- Every cleaned value sits next to its original value.
- Numbers in reports are generated by scripts, not typed by hand.
- Verified findings, interpretations and hypotheses are labelled separately.
- Notification counts are not treated as defect rates, market shares or national safety rankings.
- AI-assisted work is disclosed; machine-generated labels are never presented as human ground truth.
- Nothing is pushed to a remote repository without the owner's explicit permission.
